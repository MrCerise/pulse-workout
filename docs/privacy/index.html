Privacy policy

Interval Coach · Last updated: 20 October 2026

Who is responsible and how to contact us

This policy covers Interval Coach for Android and Wear OS, maintained by Mr Cerise (MrCerise on GitHub). For privacy questions, data rights requests or security concerns, email security@nexalab.fr. Do not send workout histories, recordings, health records or precise routes unless necessary for your request. If you contact us, we handle your message and address only to respond and follow up, and delete them when no longer needed, subject to applicable legal obligations.

Data stored by the app

Interval Coach stores workout plans, folders, labels, reminders, preferences, notes, session history and session statistics in private app storage. Running sessions can include GPS coordinates, distance, speed and route history. Voice Studio stores microphone recordings and references to media files you select. The watch stores its library, preferences, up to 20 recent local sessions and sessions awaiting delivery to your phone. Interval Coach has no user account, advertising, analytics or developer-operated backend, and does not sell personal or health data.

Location and microphone

Location is optional and used only during a running session to measure distance, speed and route, including with the screen off. Tracking stops when the session ends. Without access, the app uses estimates from your plan. Microphone access is optional and used only when you start recording coaching clips in Voice Studio. Interval Coach does not record conversations in the background. Revoke these permissions in Android app settings. Delete a route by deleting its session; remove recorded clips in Voice & audio.

Health Connect

With your permission, Interval Coach writes completed exercise sessions with interval segments to Health Connect. Interval Coach does not read health records, including heart rate or steps. Health Connect stores data on the device and controls access by other apps. Apps you separately authorize there may read the exercise records Interval Coach writes and handle them under their own policies. Interval Coach does not use health data for advertising, profiling for advertising, credit decisions or data brokerage. Manage and revoke permissions, and delete records already written, in Android Health Connect settings. Turning off sync or deleting Interval Coach history does not erase Health Connect records.

Phone and watch synchronization

When enabled, Google Play services Wearable Data Layer transfers the workout library, synchronized preferences and session information between your phone and watch. Depending on connectivity, it uses Bluetooth or a relay through Google servers; cloud transfers are end-to-end encrypted. Disable Sync with phone in watch settings to stop this link. Previously synchronized copies remain on each device until deleted there or the app data is cleared. Interval Coach does not operate the relay servers.

Exports, sharing and other services

Workout files, summary images and AI prompts leave Interval Coach only when you choose a destination through Android sharing, the file picker or clipboard. Workout QR codes contain the workout itself (its name, intervals and notes) and nothing about you. Scanning a code with the camera uses the Google Play services code scanner: the image is processed by Google Play services and Interval Coach receives only the decoded text, without camera access of its own. Importing a code from an image reads the picture you pick on the device. Summary images can include workout statistics and a route map: share them carefully. Interval Coach does not call an AI provider; a provider you choose receives the text you send and applies its own policy. Your selected text-to-speech engine may process coaching text online according to its settings and policy. Android, Health Connect, Google Play services, backup providers and selected file or sharing apps operate under their respective policies. Interval Coach itself sends no analytics or crash reports.

Backups and security

Android app-private storage and the device security features protect local data; keep your device updated and locked. Interval Coach does not add a separate encryption layer to its database or exported files. Depending on your Android settings, Android backup can upload the database and preferences, including saved workout history and routes, to your backup provider. Cloud backup excludes Interval Coach recordings and the share and backup folders; device-to-device transfer can include recordings. Files you export are protected by the destination you choose, not by Interval Coach. Control cloud backups and delete retained cloud copies through Android and your backup provider.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Phone data stays until you delete it, clear app storage or uninstall. Settings → Delete all sessions removes phone session history; individual sessions and workouts can also be deleted in their screens. Voice & audio lets you remove clips. Watch History lets you clear local sessions. Clear app storage or uninstall to remove all local Interval Coach data. Android/cloud backups, exports, Health Connect records, shared files and copies on another device are separate: delete those at their respective locations. Revoking a permission stops future access but does not delete existing copies. There is no Interval Coach account to close. For questions about your choices or applicable data rights, contact security@nexalab.fr.

Fitness use and policy updates

Interval Coach is a fitness coaching and interval timer app. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. Consult a healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Measurements may be approximate. We update this policy when practices change and display the revision date above. The policy is available offline in the app and as a public HTML page.
